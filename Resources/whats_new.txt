- A new launch animation: the Y and M fly in and lock together to make the logo.
- Film and series pages, Netflix-style: Resume with its progress, Play From Beginning and Episodes & More, or Play and Audio & Subtitles for something new. Something you're part way through plays behind the page.
- Home: rest the pointer on a film or series and it widens and plays a preview (Settings → Home Screen to turn it off).
- The player, Netflix-style: Back, Play From Beginning, Next Episode and an Episodes menu along the top. The title shows at the top right while paused.
- Skip Intro and Skip Recap now also use SkipDB and IntroDB when TheIntroDB has nothing. The captions menu shows what was found for each episode.
- Because you watched: only titles you actually watched, and right-click a card for "Don't Recommend Based on…".
- Categories: swipe left on a category with two fingers to hide it.
- AirPlay and Chromecast: watch any film, episode or channel on your Apple TV, AirPlay TV, Chromecast or Google TV. Keep the Mac awake and YM Player open while you watch.
- New look to match YM Player on TV and phone, light and dark mode, picture shapes, trailers in the app, subtitle styles and automatic updates.
