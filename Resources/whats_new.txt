- New look: the same colours as YM Player on TV and phone, with the glowing background that changes colour for each section, and smooth slides and fades as you move around.
- Light and dark: the app now follows your Mac's light or dark mode. Choose Light or Dark instead in Settings → Appearance.
- Picture: the new picture button in the player lets you choose Fit, Fill Screen, Stretch, 16:9 or 4:3, remembered for each channel, film and series. Or pinch the trackpad.
- Trailers now play right here in the app.
- Subtitles: choose their size, colour and background in Settings.
- Home Screen: choose which rows show in Settings. When there's nothing to base "Because you watched" on, you get "Recommended for You" instead.
- Frozen picture: if the picture freezes while the sound carries on, the stream reconnects by itself.
- Updates: YM Player now updates itself. It tells you when a new version is out and installs it for you.
- A quick tour for new users, and Settings → Show Tutorial to see it again.
- Fixed: signing in to providers with an http:// address failed with an "App Transport Security" error.
